IEEE Outstanding Student Volunteer Award

Presented by IEEE, the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers, to student volunteers whose work goes beyond their formal role, this award recognises sustained, hands-on contribution to a student branch rather than a single project or event.

IEEE is the world's largest technical professional organisation, spanning engineering, computing and technology, with a global membership and a name that carries real weight in the field. An award from it isn't a campus formality, it's an outside, internationally recognised body vouching for the work, which is what makes it the recognition I'm proudest of.
